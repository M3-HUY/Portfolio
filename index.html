About Me

I am a Computer Science student with a strong interest in International Affairs and Law. I enjoy learning how technology and global policy connect because I believe good software should solve real problems and work within the laws and regulations of the world.

I like building simple, practical, and reliable web applications while also learning about international policy, cybersecurity, Law and data protection. Studying both fields helps me look at problems from different perspectives and create solutions that are not only technically effective but also realistic and responsible.

My goal is to keep improving my technical skills, expand my understanding of global issues, and build technology that people can trust. I believe the best solutions are simple, useful, secure, and designed to make life easier for others.
